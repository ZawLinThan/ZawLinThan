# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Zaw Lin Than for internships or co-ops, and potential collaborators evaluating his work. Both audiences are equally important.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that presents Zaw Lin Than’s work, education, experience, leadership, community involvement, and skills so visitors can understand his background and connect with him about opportunities or collaboration.

## Positioning

Zaw Lin Than is a Georgia Tech computer science student focused on AI and information internetworks, building AI tools and full-stack applications. The portfolio presents this work alongside leadership, employment, and community involvement.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse projects, résumé, education, skills, experience, leadership, and community involvement, then may contact Zaw by email or through GitHub and LinkedIn. The site states that he is open to internships and co-ops.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The site is a React web application with routes for home, about, projects, project details, skills, experience, and contact. It includes project galleries, an embedded résumé viewer and download, and external profile links. The current content identifies Georgia Tech as his school and Atlanta, Georgia as his location. Keep educational, employment, leadership, and project facts accurate to their source material.

## Product Principles

- Represent education, work, leadership, and community involvement accurately.
- Make projects and skills easy to evaluate.
- Serve opportunity seekers and potential collaborators equally.
- Provide clear paths to the résumé and contact channels.
